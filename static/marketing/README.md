# Marketing assets

`cafe.webp` (1200 × 800) and `cafe-small.webp` (640 × 427) are optimized versions of an original image generated for Serveza with the built-in ImageGen tool. The scene illustrates a café. The hero dashboard, phone, kitchen ticket and data visualizations are HTML, CSS and SVG built from fictional records. The phone uses a resized copy of the existing repository demo dish photograph.

`social.jpg` is a 1200 × 630 screenshot composition of Serveza's logo, headline and actual responsive hero illustration, rendered in local Chrome. Brand marks retain the source artwork and proportions.

## Café image prompt

Photorealistic hospitality editorial photograph, wide horizontal 3:2. A close overhead three-quarter still life on the edge of a walnut cafe table in an independent contemporary Cairo espresso bar. A single ivory latte cup with detailed rosetta on saucer, a crisp croissant on a small ceramic plate and a neatly folded petrol-teal linen napkin. Warm angled morning sun illuminating the coffee foam and flaky layers; subtle geometric window shadows, natural film grain. Out of focus curved bentwood chair and tiled floor. Tactile high-end printed hospitality magazine, documentary detail, intentional asymmetrical crop, warm amber and navy tones, no people, text, logos, devices, lettering or QR codes.
